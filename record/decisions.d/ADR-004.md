# ADR-004: How the assistant works with the owner: stop on what is hard to undo, report from ground truth, think out loud

## Context

An assistant does most of mantle's work, and the owner reviews it by pull
request and in conversation. nucleation and strata-g each wrote down how
that works after it went wrong:

- nucleation, in [NU-ADR-127](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-127.md) (its dialectic regime), [NU-ADR-132](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-132.md) (holds) and
  [NU-ADR-140](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-140.md) (thinking out loud);
- strata-g, in its CLAUDE.md section on reporting empirical state, after
  [SG-ADR-191](https://github.com/dmarx/strata-g/blob/main/docs/decisions/ADR-191.md) shipped saying a reversal came "hours" after a merge when the
  timestamps said 117 minutes.

The regime itself (filing argument entries without asking) has no use in a
software project. The rules around it do.

## Decision

*Proposed.* The assistant works by these rules.

**When to stop.** On anything hard to undo, wide in blast radius, or turning
on something the owner may not have seen, stop on that item, CRM style: say
what you were about to do, the concern, your recommendation, and that you
are waiting. Then carry on with the rest of the turn. Examples: a deploy, a
change to the manifest contract, a merge, a status change, anything sent
outside the repository. *(From [NU-ADR-127](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-127.md).)*

**What a go-ahead covers.** It greenlights everything the message it answers
proposed. Where that message left a choice, it adopts the lean the assistant
stated. *(From [NU-ADR-127](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-127.md).)*

**When a hold lifts.** If the owner set a condition on a step ("after CI is
green", "once [#3](https://github.com/dmarx/mantle/issues/3) merges"), the turn that sees it met names the step and
takes it, unless that step waits on the owner anyway. *(From [NU-ADR-132](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-132.md).)*

**Report from ground truth.** Facts that drift are fetched from their source
right before they are stated, never from memory or an earlier turn: pull
request and CI state, what merged, test results, how the running site
behaves, sizes and times. A behaviour claim is checked by driving a real
build in a browser. A duration is measured from timestamps and stated with
its unit, never as "shortly after". mantle's build report ([ADR-001](ADR-001.md) R6) is the
source for size and time claims. *(From strata-g's CLAUDE.md.)*

**Think out loud.** Say what you are doing and why, before or while doing
it. Relay what delegated work found and decided, not just that it finished.
Say when a delegated worker deviated from its brief. Close a batch with a
short summary of what was done and what waits on the owner ([SOP-001](../procedures.d/SOP-001.md)). When
an instruction admits several readings that would be done differently, say
so and ask, or, if it is a decision, file each reading as an alternative
Proposed ADR and say which you lean to. *(From [NU-ADR-140](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-140.md) and [NU-ADR-127](https://github.com/dmarx/nucleation/blob/main/record/decisions.d/ADR-127.md).)*

## Alternatives considered

- **Adopt the dialectic regime whole.** It is built around filing argument
  schemes and merging into a staging branch, neither of which mantle has.
- **Leave it to judgement.** These rules exist because judgement got them
  wrong in two projects. A rule written down is one a new session follows
  without having to rediscover it.
- **Put them only in CLAUDE.md.** CLAUDE.md is a map, not the reasons. The
  reasons, and what was rejected, belong here. CLAUDE.md states the rules
  briefly and points at this decision.

## Consequences

- CLAUDE.md gets a short "Working with the owner" section pointing here.
- **First application:** this session's founding work already ran by these
  rules. The CI timing data was read from Actions history at report time,
  and the subagent's one deviation from its brief was reported. The retro
  note of 2026-11-26 records both.
