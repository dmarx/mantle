# DP-014: Pilot a change on a sample before sweeping

When a change will land on many things, apply it to a small sample first,
let the owner look, and then sweep.

Why: a rule can be read and agreed with and still come out wrong in
application. Its wording describes what the author pictured, and the things
it lands on vary more than that. The sample is where the gap shows, while it
is cheap to fix.

Applied here:

- **Records move onto mantle smallest first**: luria's own record, then
  strata-g's or mantle's, and nucleation's last. Each move is checked
  against the published Quartz site it replaces before the next one starts.
- A manifest schema change is applied to the fixtures and one real producer
  before the others are asked to follow.
